Read root AGENTS.md and docs/AGENT-OPERATIONS.md before running a job. Resolve the root by workflow.py, not a machine-specific path. Use docs/SETUP-AGENT.md for installation; never run paid inference during setup. Consult docs/OPENART.md, docs/HF-RAW-MASKS.md and the live OpenArt schema for provider contracts. docs/DREAMINA.md covers historical job recovery only.

Use workflow.py and the local UI. Preserve source media and credentials. ElevenLabs isolates/transcribes audio; guidance defaults to Rubber Band +3 semitones or explicitly configured STS. Embed guidance in the prepared video. Final exports restore all original audio packets by default; explicitly configured STS exports use converted voice plus estimated residual background and must be described accurately.

Depth and SAM run in Hugging Face cloud. Preserve colored depth and require actual binary raw PNG masks. Overlay-only Spaces are unsupported. Retry masks on original RGB when needed, retain the colored depth, and record fallback. Full-clip visual QA is mandatory; inspect cuts, hair, limbs, clothes, spill, holes, alignment and flicker. Sampled OpenAI advice never grants approval. Bind approval to current artifact hashes; user review may be recorded without claiming agent inspection.

Fast face_mesh skips depth/SAM and uses the isolated CPU worker. Combined depth_mesh retains the depth composite and adds original-frame face tracking. Inspect all frames; both mesh modes are experimental for one centered speaker. Install the worker with Python 3.12 and setup-face-mesh.sh; its small Google model downloads on first use. Review facial tracking and audio in fast mode, masks plus tracking in combined mode. Prompts must remove all guidance lines/colors, replace the full identity including hair and preserve dialogue/timing.

Show the exact creative prompt and actual mode. OpenArt uses Seedance 2.5 Draft (`byte-plus-seedance-2-5-draft`), `element2video`, 480p, with the prepared source first and appearance references after it. The official CLI owns OAuth; its limited video flags are not used to fake reference-video support. The headless app uses OpenArt's official MCP service and direct upload contract. Video identities remain videos; never turn them into pictures without permission or take their voice/performance by default. HD actions remain disabled locally until a separate quoted and explicitly approved lineage flow is implemented.

Persist request identity, artifact hashes and an attempted marker before generation, then the actual provider history ID when returned. Never blindly retry uncertain runs, purchase credits or approve a quote without explicit authorization. OpenArt does not expose server-side idempotency or a maximum-charge field, so the fresh quote gate is local. Retain old Dreamina jobs for collection only. Preserve draft artifacts, obtain an actual downloaded result, restore the full audio and show preview/download before claiming delivery. Keep preview selection stable and never expose tokens, signed URLs or credentials.
